# Constitution guidance

Read the [constitution](../../CONSTITUTION.md) before changing governed work or proposing an amendment. It owns the constraints, approval authority, and amendment procedure.

## Apply the rules

Distinguish durable constraints from implementation choices and feature requirements. Do not infer new policy from current code.

Check proposed changes against relevant principles. Surface conflicts and stop affected work until the owner resolves them. An agent proposal does not amend governance.

When proposing a rule, state the constraint and its observable compliance signal. Preserve existing policy owners and follow the constitution's approval procedure.

## Reconcile and verify

Reconcile directly affected consumer docs, specifications, plans, agent instructions, and checks. Verify changed paths, links, commands, and claims against their owners.

Report governance changes, unresolved decisions, and verification limits. Establishing governance does not authorize further development.

## Maintenance

Keep this guide and discovery links current when owners move. Changes to this procedure require owner approval; constitutional amendments follow the constitution's own rules.
